# Концертный фон

Файл проекта: public/images/concert.webp. Создан встроенным imagegen, затем перекодирован в WebP без изменения композиции. Изображение иллюстративное, не документальное фото реальной Thunder bend.

## Финальный prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: photographic hero backdrop for a fictional music band website. Create a cinematic horizontal concert photograph, wide 3:2 landscape frame, seen from within a small intimate concert venue. Three young adult indie rock musicians on stage, vocalist with guitar in center, guitarist right, drummer further back, mostly atmospheric silhouettes, believable instruments and anatomy. Low-key near-black stage, hazy beams of pale warm light and subtle moss-green lighting, analog 35mm film grain, expressive energetic but restrained editorial composition. Full-bleed concert image, musicians and light concentrated on the right half, left half dark with useful negative space for large white website copy. No lettering, no words, no logos, no watermarks, no frame, no UI. Authentic candid live music atmosphere, not a poster or illustration.
